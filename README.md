# Tunas Routine

A cute, gamified daily routine tracker that you can install on your phone. Tunas the sprout cheers you on while you tick off your day.

- **Today as game levels:** each part of your day (Morning, After work, Workout, Focus, Night, This week) is a level that earns up to 3 stars. Tunas stands on the level to play next.
- **XP, levels and streaks:** every tick earns XP; hit 80% of your day to keep your streak
- **Level rewards:** every new level gives a gift once: even levels a Rare or better find, odd levels a streak shield (or a find if you already hold 2), and levels 2 to 10 a new title. The Progress card shows what the next level unlocks
- **Daily quests, 16 badges and a daily chest:** the ★ quest stays, the other two change every day; finish them all to open the chest and keep a find (28 to collect, from common to epic) in the Me tab. Find the same one 3 times and it turns shiny, so repeats still count. Four of them are seasonal Rares: Monsoon Umbrella (Nov–Jan), Wau Bulan Kite (Feb–May), Durian (Jun–Aug) and Paper Lantern (Sep–Oct). While one is in season and not found yet, it is your next Rare, and each comes back every year, so nobody misses one for good
- **Habit medals:** every day a habit is ticked counts toward its medal: Bronze at 7 days, Silver at 30, Gold at 100. The days don't need to be in a row, so a missed day never takes one away. Medals show in Progress → Habit by habit
- **Since you started:** Progress shows the day you started with Tunas, how many days it's been and your total ticks. Week summaries and habit stats only count days from then, so days before you started are never "missed"
- **Workout logger:** log kg × reps per set, get "NEW PR" when you beat your best, plus a rest timer that beeps and vibrates
- **Celebration screens, sounds and haptics** when you clear a section or have a perfect day
- **Money (Me → Money):** add what comes in each month and jot spends by category (food, transport, bills, shopping and more). See what's left, a monthly report of where it went, past months, and short, kind tips from Tunas. Any currency (Rupiah shows as Rp 1.500.000), with shortcuts like 25rb, 2k or 1.5jt. Stays on your phone and is part of your backup
- **History calendar:** tap any past day to see it or fix a tick you forgot
- **Phone reminders:** add each section to Google Calendar in one tap, or download an `.ics` file
- **Tick all:** finish a whole section in one tap
- **Count habits:** give a habit a target like 8 glasses of water and tap +1 each time
- **Focus timer:** start a 25-minute focus round from the focus-hour habit; Tunas ticks it at zero and gives you a 5-minute break
- **Rest days:** protect your streak when you're sick or busy (2 per month)
- **Streak shields:** chests sometimes drop a shield (hold up to 2) that covers a missed day automatically. If the streak restarts anyway, the shield comes back to you
- **Weekly chest:** reach your goal (80% done, or a rest day) on 5 of 7 days and a bigger chest opens with last week's summary the first time you open Tunas in the new week (any day, not just Monday): 2 finds, one Rare or better for sure, plus a streak shield if you have room
- **Starter trail:** new players get a 7-stop trail for their first week, one small goal a day (tick 3 habits, finish a section, open the chest...). Stop 4 gives a streak shield and stop 7 a Rare or better find. Missing a day just pauses it
- **Evening check-in (optional, off by default):** turn it on in Routine > Reminders. From 4pm, Today offers one tap to add a reminder to your calendar for the habits still open tonight. It's a calendar link like the other reminders, so nothing is sent anywhere
- **Collection sets:** the collection book groups every find into 6 themed sets (Garden, Forest, Seaside, Night sky, Treasure, Seasons). Completing a set unlocks a Tunas outfit
- **Comeback bonus:** back after 2+ quiet days? Tunas says welcome back instead of showing a zero, and ticking 3 habits that first day gives +60 XP
- **Continue button and home-screen shortcuts:** long-press the app icon for Today, Workout, Night or History
- **A page for every tab:** Routine, Progress, Me and Settings are full pages. Each one shows its main controls up front and opens bigger groups (your week, packs, reminders, backup, badges, chest finds...) as their own pages with a back arrow, so nothing hides in a dropdown
- **Routine editor:** add, rename, reorder and delete sections, habits, workouts and weekly tasks right in the app
- **Welcome screen and 4 templates:** Student, Worker, Fitness and Simple, so new users start in seconds
- **English and Bahasa Melayu:** switch anytime in the Settings tab
- **Easy on the eyes:** pick light, dark or same-as-phone, and switch to large text in Settings → Display
- **Share button:** send the app to friends from the phone's share sheet
- **Works offline** and installs like a normal app
- **Tunas assistant:** one card on Today that changes with your day: a morning plan, Now / Next / Later during the day, a Rough day? check-in that shrinks today to the must-dos that keep your streak, and an evening wrap-up with one tip for tomorrow. All rules on your phone, and it can be turned off in Settings
- **Workout check-in:** can't train today? Tell Tunas it's raining, you're home late or you're tired, and get a plan B (indoor, 10-minute mini, or stretch and rest) that still counts. With the optional rain check on, real rain at your workout time keeps your streak safe
- **Private:** no account, no server. Everything is saved on your phone only. The one exception is the optional rain check (off by default): on your tap it sends your rough area, rounded to about 10 km, to the free Open-Meteo weather service. Your location is never saved

## Install on your phone

Open **https://tunas.amkaz.dev** on your phone, then:

- **Android (Chrome):** tap the **⋮** menu → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**)
- **iPhone (Safari):** tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**

> **iPhone tip:** keep Tunas on the Home Screen. In a Safari tab (or inside Instagram, WhatsApp and other apps) iOS can clear a site's data after about a week without a visit, so the routine would be gone. Tunas shows a card on iPhone until it runs from the Home Screen. Safari and the Home Screen app keep separate data, so tap **Copy my data** in Safari, then **Paste my data** in the Home Screen app (on its first screen, or Settings → Backup) to bring everything across. Nothing leaves the phone.

> **Moved from `giyuuln.github.io/tunas-routine`?** Each web address keeps its own storage, so your data doesn't follow by itself. Open the old address, tap **Download backup** on the banner, then open tunas.amkaz.dev, tap **I have a Tunas backup** and pick the file.

## Make it yours

On first open, Tunas asks your name and language, then what you want help with. Pick a few **habit packs** (30 to choose from: skincare, water, sleep, solat, study, money, pet care and more), tell it when you wake up, go to bed and work or study, and it builds your routine on the phone. Pick **Skincare** and it asks your skin type, how many steps you want (Basic, Standard or Full) and what you want to work on, then builds morning and night steps with serums and treatment nights that fit. Pick **Workouts** and it also asks your level (newbie, intermediate or regular), home or gym (with a suggestion for your level) and what equipment you have, then builds a plan from bodyweight, bands, dumbbells, a pull-up bar or gym machines. Prefer a full routine? Tap **Use a ready-made routine instead**. After that:

- **Routine tab → Habit packs** to add more packs or remove one (its habits, weekly tasks, tips and guide topics go; your history stays)
- **Routine tab → Edit routine** to change any section, habit, time, workout, weekly task, and the **Guide** tab for your own guide and daily tips
- **Routine tab → Start a new routine** to start over with packs or a template (your XP and streak stay)
- **Routine tab → Import routine file** if someone gave you a routine `.json` (or tap **Paste**)

The routine is saved in the app on your phone. **Download my routine** saves a copy, and **Download backup** saves your XP, streak and workout logs.

**Download locked backup** saves the same backup protected with a password (AES-GCM, encrypted on the phone), so it's safer to keep in cloud storage or send to yourself. If you forget the password, that file can't be opened. **Restore backup** opens both kinds and asks for the password when the file is locked.

`.gitignore` blocks `*-routine.json`, `*.private.json` and `personal/`, so personal files don't get committed by accident.

## Routine file format

The easiest way to get a routine file is **Routine tab → Download my routine** after editing in the app. The templates in [`templates.js`](templates.js) use the same structure, with `{ "en": …, "ms": … }` pairs for both languages.

```json
{
  "version": 1,
  "label": "My routine",
  "name": "Your name",
  "blocks": [
    {
      "id": "morning", "title": "Morning", "icon": "sun",
      "from": "05:00", "to": "08:30", "range": "6:30 – 8:00 am",
      "say": ["What Tunas says during this time", "Smaller second line"],
      "items": [
        { "id": "spf", "title": "Sunscreen", "note": "Optional detail", "time": "7:30", "quest": true }
      ]
    }
  ],
  "focus":    { "0": { "title": "1 hour: plan the week", "note": "" } },
  "workouts": { "3": { "name": "Full Body", "when": "6:15 pm", "from": "18:00", "to": "19:15", "after": "evening",
                       "exercises": [ { "id": "squat", "name": "Goblet Squat", "reps": "10–12", "rest": "90s", "cue": "3 seconds down" } ] } },
  "progression": { "squat": { "start": "10 kg", "step": 2, "max": 18 } },
  "weekly": [ { "id": "pillow", "title": "Change pillowcase" } ],
  "away": { "from": "08:30", "to": "17:30", "say": ["Good luck at work!", "Drink water."] },
  "tips": [ "**Bold** works in tips and guide lines" ],
  "guide": { "title": "Guide", "sections": [ { "title": "Section", "lines": [ "**Morning:** …" ] } ] },
  "packs": [ "skin", "water" ],
  "day": { "wake": "06:30", "sleep": "23:00", "busy": [ "08:30", "17:00" ] },
  "fitness": { "level": "mid", "place": "home", "gear": [ "db", "band" ] },
  "skin": { "type": "combo", "level": "std", "concerns": [ "acne" ] }
}
```

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `icon` | `sun`, `walk`, `focus`, `moon`, `dumb`, `cal`, `book`, `heart`, `star` |
| `from` / `to` | When the section is "now" (24h). If `to` is earlier than `from`, it runs past midnight |
| `quest: true` | A ★ habit: worth more XP and part of the daily quest |
| `everyOtherDay: true` | Shows only every second day |
| `target: 8` | A count habit: tap +1 each time (with a − button to take one off); it ticks when it reaches the target (2–99) |
| `pack` | On habits, weekly tasks, workouts and guide sections: which pack added it, so removing the pack removes it. Leave it out for your own items |
| `packs` / `day` | Packs in use, and the wake/sleep/busy hours used to place habits from new packs (see [`packs.js`](packs.js)) |
| `fitness` | Workout setup: `level` `new`/`mid`/`pro`, `place` `home`/`gym`, `gear` any of `db`, `band`, `bar`. **Routine tab → Habit packs → Change workout setup** rebuilds the pack's workouts; logs stay |
| `skin` | Skincare setup: `type` `oily`/`dry`/`combo`/`normal`/`sensitive`/`unsure`, `level` `basic`/`std`/`full`, `concerns` any of `acne`, `spots`, `dull`, `dry`, `oil`, `lines`. **Change skincare setup** rebuilds the pack's habits; ticks stay |
| `onDays` (habit) | Show only on these weekdays, `0` = Sunday (e.g. retinoid nights `[1, 3, 6]`) |
| `days` (habit) | `"work"` or `"off"`: show only on work/class days or days off (from `away.days`). Ignored when there are no fixed hours |
| `away.days` | Work or class days for the fixed hours, `0` = Sunday. Default Monday to Friday. Tunas only says its "at work" lines on these days |
| `sets` (workout) | Sets per exercise (1–5). Without it: 2 sets for the first 4 weeks, then 3 |
| `tips` / `guide` | Up to 50 tips and 12 guide sections of 20 lines each |
| `hideOnDays: [3]` | Hide on these weekdays (0 = Sunday … 6 = Saturday) |
| `useFocus: true` | Title comes from the `focus` entry for today's weekday |
| `workouts` | Keyed by weekday. `after` is the section id it appears after in Today's levels |
| `bodyweight: true`, `unit: "sec"` | Exercise logs reps only (or seconds) |
| `progression` | Per-exercise start weight and how much to add when you hit the top reps |
| `remind` | Optional `"HH:MM"` on a section or workout, used for calendar reminders (defaults to `from`) |

## XP rules

| Action | XP |
|---|---|
| Habit | +10 |
| ★ habit | +20 |
| Workout done | +50 |
| All ★ habits (daily quest) | +40 |
| Perfect day | +50 |
| New personal record | +25 |
| Weekly task | +15 |

A day counts toward your streak at 80% or more, when you mark it as a rest day, or when a streak shield covers a missed day. A new day starts at 4am, so late nights count for the day before.

Each past day keeps the habits it had that day. Adding habits, removing a pack or starting a new routine never takes back a streak, XP, a level or a badge you already earned. Ticking a habit on a past day can only help that day.

## Translations

All interface text is in [`i18n.js`](i18n.js). To add a language, copy the `en` block, translate it, and add a button for it in `segLang()` in `app.js`.

## Develop

It's plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no build step.

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000   # then open http://localhost:8000
```

When you change app files, bump `VERSION` in `sw.js` so installed phones pick up the update.

## Privacy

Tunas has no account, no server and no tracking. Your routine, ticks, XP and workout logs are saved only in your phone's browser storage (`localStorage`), so the developer never receives or sees anyone's data. A Content-Security-Policy in `index.html` stops the app from sending data to any outside website. Use **Settings → Download backup** to keep your own copy.
